# QuoteHarbor launch material

## Community post

Title: Save the quote. Keep the source.

I built QuoteHarbor, a Chrome extension for a simpler everyday browsing workflow.

QuoteHarbor saves selected text from web pages together with its source title and link. Keep useful quotes for research, reading, or writing, then find them in a searchable local library.

What it does:
- Preview and save selected web text
- Search quotes, notes, and tags
- Export Markdown or JSON backups

Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kaflppcnmmcejmdhcnicjhoaogagjgbd

I am the developer. I would appreciate feedback on the workflow, confusing steps, or missing essentials. Please share your own experience; there is no reward or required rating.
